Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who We Are

YearsYoga LLC ("we," "us," or "our") provides chair yoga, face yoga, and gentle yoga classes for seniors, both in person and online. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or enroll in our classes.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details — name, email address, and phone number when you book a class or contact us.
  • Class registration details — attendance records and any accessibility needs you voluntarily share.
  • Payment information — processed securely by our payment provider; we never store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical data — basic browser and device information used to keep the site secure and working properly.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, confirm, and deliver your classes.
  • To send class reminders, schedule updates, and occasional wellness tips (you may unsubscribe at any time).
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To improve our website and services.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only with trusted service providers (such as payment processors and video-conferencing platforms) as needed to run our classes, and only to the extent required by law if legally compelled.

5. Health Information

If you share health or mobility information so we can adapt poses for you, we treat it as confidential and use it only to keep your practice safe. We are not a covered entity under HIPAA, but we follow similar care with your sensitive information.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and secure payment processing — to protect your information.

7. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality. We do not use advertising trackers.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence (including rights granted under the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to opt out of marketing communications. To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@yearsyoga.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version.
