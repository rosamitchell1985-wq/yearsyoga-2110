Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 2026

Our Commitment

YearsYoga is committed to ensuring our website and classes are accessible to everyone, including seniors and people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What We Do

  • High-contrast color schemes with large, readable text.
  • Keyboard navigation support throughout the site.
  • Descriptive text alternatives (alt text) on all images and illustrations.
  • Semantic HTML so screen readers can interpret content correctly.
  • Captions and verbal cueing in all online classes — every pose is described aloud, so visual impairment is never a barrier.

Assistance with Online Classes

We offer a free 10-minute technology check before your first online class and can walk you through joining step by step by phone. Large-print class handouts are available on request.

Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our website or in a class, please tell us — we want to fix it. Contact us at access@yearsyoga.com or (555) 214-6789 and we will respond within 2 business days.
