Health & Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: October 2026

Important: Please read this disclaimer carefully before participating in any YearsYoga class.

1. General Wellness Only

All classes, exercises, and content provided by YearsYoga LLC are offered for general health and wellness purposes. Nothing on this website or in our classes constitutes medical advice, physical therapy, diagnosis, or treatment of any kind.

2. Consult Your Physician

We strongly recommend that you consult your doctor or qualified healthcare provider before beginning any exercise program — especially if you have or suspect you have any medical condition, including heart disease, high blood pressure, osteoporosis, arthritis, recent surgery, balance disorders, or any other condition that may affect your ability to exercise safely.

3. Listen to Your Body

Never force or strain during any movement. Yoga should never cause pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, or shortness of breath. If you experience any of these, stop immediately and seek medical attention if symptoms persist.

4. Face Yoga Specific Notice

Face yoga involves gentle movements and self-massage of the face and neck. It is not a cosmetic medical procedure and does not replace the advice of a dermatologist or physician. Results vary from person to person.

5. No Guarantees

While many students report improvements in mobility, balance, relaxation, and overall well-being, individual results vary. We make no guarantees regarding specific health outcomes.

6. Emergency Situations

Our online instructors cannot see or respond to your home environment. In an emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) immediately. Do not rely on our staff for emergency assistance during remote classes.
